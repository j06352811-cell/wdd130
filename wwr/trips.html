Choose Your Rapids! Adventure

Compare the routes below, then contact our team to discuss dates, group needs, and current river conditions.

Los Dientes del Diablo

Our most challenging listed route follows a string of technical rapids through a narrow canyon. Expect active paddling, splashy drops, and scenic breaks between sections. A good fit for adventurous guests comfortable following guide instructions.

Plan for: Full day · Advanced · Ages 16+

Big Mallard

A lively half-day run with a mix of easy paddling and moderate rapids. Guides introduce river signals and help the crew find a steady rhythm, with time to enjoy the wooded banks along the way.

Plan for: Half day · Intermediate · Ages 12+

Half Day Adventure

This shorter outing is a friendly introduction to rafting. After a safety briefing, the crew works through gentle moving water with a few small splashy sections. It leaves room in the day for other plans.

Plan for: 3 hours · Beginner · Ages 8+

River Run Escape

Spend a relaxed half day on a scenic stretch with calm pools and occasional wave trains. It is a balanced choice for mixed-experience groups who want both river time and quieter moments.

Plan for: Half day · Beginner to intermediate · Ages 10+

Rapid Canyon Run

Follow a canyon route with sustained paddling and several brisk rapids. The guide sets the pace and shares a safety talk before launch; guests should be ready to work as a team throughout the run.

Plan for: 5 hours · Intermediate to advanced · Ages 14+
